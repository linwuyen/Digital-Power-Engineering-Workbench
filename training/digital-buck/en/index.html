PREVIEW 1 · PHYSICS

If only L changes, what happens to inductor ripple?

Get the direction before the formula.

48 → 12 V, 100 kHz, ideal CCM. Increasing L reduces current ripple. This answers a physical energy-flow question; it does not prove closed-loop performance.
